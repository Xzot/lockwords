Art of the Day

Quiz yourself on a painting, right on your Home Screen.

The widget shows today's artwork and asks a question about it: who made it, when, or what it shows. Tap an answer on the widget itself. The title, the artist, the year and your streak appear straight away, and the full story waits in the app. Quiz buttons are on the medium and large Home Screen widgets.

  • Three answers, one tap, no need to open the app
  • Every work credited to the museum that holds it
  • Stories translated into 28 languages
Sky & Day

The Moon, every day.

See tonight's phase at a glance on your Lock Screen, with sunrise, sunset and the change in daylight for your city.

  • The Moon drawn from NASA lunar imagery, in tonight's true phase
  • Type a city, or use your approximate location if you like
  • Round, rectangular and one-line widgets above or under the clock

Two minutes to set up

Put it under your clock.

The Widgets tab in the app shows every widget with your content and walks you through it. Here is the short version.

Choose what to see

Open LockWords and pick your card packs in the Library. Art of the Day and Sky & Day are widgets of their own.

Customize your Lock Screen

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen, and tap the widget area under the clock.

Add LockWords

Find LockWords in the list and tap the widget you want. Tap Done. On the Home Screen it's Edit, then Add Widget.

Step-by-step help for every widget

Pay once. Keep it.

Affirmations and the Impressionism art pack come with the app, and every other pack lets you try its first cards. Buy a single pack, a whole collection, or All Packs, which also includes every pack added later. Purchases work with Family Sharing and restore on any device with your Apple ID.

  • No subscription and no renewals
  • Sky & Day included
  • All Packs owners get the Art packs too

Private by design.

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your packs, saved cards, quiz answers and the place you pick for Sky & Day stay on your iPhone. Location is optional and asked for only when you tap "Use my approximate location".

  • Privacy label: Data Not Collected
  • Works offline
  • Read the privacy policy
Help & contact

Questions people ask.

How do I add the widget to my Lock Screen?

Touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen. Tap the widget area under the clock, find LockWords and pick a widget. Tap Done. Full steps

How do I answer the painting quiz?

Add the medium or large Art of the Day widget to your Home Screen. Tap one of the three answers on the widget. The answer, the year and your streak show up right away.

Is there a subscription?

No. Everything is a one-time purchase, and Sky & Day is included with the app.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID. More about purchases

Privacy Policy

Short version: LockWords collects nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Effective with LockWords 2.0 (October 2026)

LockWords (“the app”) is developed by Vladyslav Shchuka (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

We don't collect your data

The app does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Your settings are stored only on your device, in storage shared between the app and its widgets. That includes your selected packs and collections, rotation frequency, saved cards and artworks, the words you marked as known, your quiz answers and streak, and the place you pick for Sky & Day. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Location (optional)

Sky & Day can show the Moon, sunrise and sunset for your place. You can type a city, or tap “Use my approximate location”, which asks iOS for approximate location while you use the app. The app asks only from that picker, never in the background and never from a widget. We store the place name and its coordinates, rounded to about 11 km, on your device only. We never receive them, and there is no tracking. You can remove the place in Sky & Day at any time. The Moon phase and week number work without a place.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app only asks Apple which purchases your Apple ID owns, so it can unlock content. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Network

The app works offline. It connects to the internet in two cases. First, to load prices, complete purchases and restore them through Apple's StoreKit. Second, when you search for a city or use your approximate location in Sky & Day: Apple Maps services receive the text you type or your approximate coordinates to return a place name. That exchange is between your device and Apple, under Apple's Privacy Policy; we get no copy. Artwork images and texts are inside the app. Opening a museum credit link opens your browser.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

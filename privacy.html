Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

LockWords (“the app”) is developed by Vladyslav Shchuka (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

We don't collect your data

The app does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Your settings are stored only on your device, in storage shared between the app and its widgets. That includes your selected packs, rotation frequency, saved cards and the words you marked as known. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app only asks Apple which purchases your Apple ID owns, so it can unlock content. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Network

The app works offline. It connects to the internet only through Apple's StoreKit to load prices, complete purchases and restore them.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
